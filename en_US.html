Password Generator

ASCII Syllables
⚠️ random phonetic selection ⚠️
⚠️ inappropriate words may appear ⚠️ Alphanumeric Hexadecimals Numbers


The ASCII button defines that your generated password will be composed in a variety of ways, including uppercase letters, lowercase letters, symbols, and numbers.

The syllables button defines that your generated password will be longer, but with lowercase syllables separated by spaces.

The alphanumerics button defines that your generated password will consist of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, and numbers.

The hexadecimals button indicates that your password will be composed of hexadecimal numbers, from 0 to f. This is unconventional in everyday use, but has technical applications.

The numbers button indicates that your password will consist only of conventional numbers, from 0 to 9.

By default, this program generates ASCII characters.
